`issue-reviewer` can now review a tracking issue together with its sub-issues. It reads the whole set in one call, and checks it against the design for three more problems: a requirement that no child covers and the parent hasn't marked end-to-end or deferred; a child that needs another child's output but isn't marked blocked by it; and a child whose approach contradicts the design section it cites. A finding that the design itself should change goes on the tracking issue. The expected fix is an edit to that issue's body plus a decision-log row, not edits to the children.
